# résumé on the fly

A small bird in a quiet, sunlit house with the doors and windows open. A bubble machine by the door never stops, and every bubble the bird pops is the next word of my resume. In a hurry? Fly to the laptop on the desk and it all arrives at once. Nothing chases you and nothing ends the game, so you can also just fly, indoors or out.

**Play it at [dhansomaiah.github.io](https://dhansomaiah.github.io).**

- **Computer:** hold Space to fly and let go to glide down and land. The mouse steers, or use the arrow keys or WASD. Shift hurries.
- **Phone:** hold a thumb down to fly, drag to steer, let go to land.

One `index.html`, no build step. The 3D is [three.js](https://threejs.org), loaded from a CDN.
